## Evidence cautions found while reviewing the recording

These are internal quality-control items, not additional professor statements:

- The presentation says the team visited both the construction site and the supplier. The archive must identify who actually visited each place and which evidence came through third parties.
- Lucas reported cracked pieces, falls during unloading, and manual carrying, but **did not report witnessing an injury**. The presentation must not attribute cutting incidents to his interview.
- Claims about deadline pressure, multiple handoffs, replacement cost, breakage frequency, and the causal dominance of transport remain reported or hypothesized until the original distributor evidence is archived.
- Foreign benchmarks and purchasing buffers cannot be presented as a Brazilian sanitary-ware breakage rate.
- The discussion of inflatable packaging and a distributor’s own patent is useful prior art, but it needs a source, documentation, and cost comparison.
